Graphite Products and Services

Print

Graphite is valued across many industries for its unique combination of high-temperature stability, resistance to thermal shock, low coefficient of thermal expansion, corrosion resistance, lubricity, and machinability. These properties make graphite an ideal material for applications where metals, ceramics, and polymers may fail.
 
Pyrotek manufactures engineered graphite products and provides advanced graphitization, carburization, and thermal purification services for customers operating in some of the most demanding industrial environments.

Industrial Graphite Products

Pyrotek facilities have been manufacturing specialty graphite products for over 60 years. Pyrotek's properietary furnace technology enables repeatable purity up to 99.995% and above. Our capabilities include:

Graphite Tubes and Rods

Graphite tube and rod stock can be ordered to your dimensional requirements:

  • Lengths up to 12 ft (3.65m)
  • Outer diameter up to 12 in(305mm)
  • Specialization in diameters between .75 in (19mm) and 4.25 in (108mm)
  • Customization possible

Pyrotek Engineered Graphite Tubes

 

Aerospace Grade Graphite

Pyrotek produces G-grade graphite (such as G-83 and G-90), which is designed for high-performance aerospace applications, such as rocket nozzles. This grade of graphite has a higher density and more uniform structure, giving it superior strength and durability while maintaining the weight and corrosion resistence benefits of graphite. Pyrotek produces G-grade graphite in a US-based facility. 

Contact a Pyrotek application engineer for more information

 

Rashig Rings

Rashig rings are commonly used in the gas, petroleum and chemical processing industries to increase surface area in reactor beds. Pyrotek produces carbon rashig rings, along with graphite and impregnated graphite raschig rings. Common dimensions include:

  • Outer diameter: 0.75 in (19mm) to 3 in (76mm)
  • Inner diameter: 0.5 in (12.7mm) to 2.37in (60.3mm) 

These Rashig rings are usable in most acid, alkali, and solvent environments.

Pyrotek carbon Raschig rings

 

Heat Exchangers and Chemical Processing

Graphite is widely used in corrosive chemical environments where metal components can experience rapid degradation. Graphite offers superb corrosion resistance, high thermal conductivity, and resistance to thermal shock while also providing long service life, dimensional stability, and excellent machinability. Pyrotek graphite tubes are commonly used in heat transfer applications such as:
 
  • Sulfuric acid heat exchangers
  • Hydrocholoric acid heat exchangers
  • Heat exchanger tube replacement/retrofits
  • Corrosive fluid handling systems
  • Chemical production facilities
Pyrotek graphite tubes and rods help heat exchanger manufacturers improve production efficiency and reduce lifecycle costs in critical process applications with consistent batch-to-batch material properties and support for custom sizes and lengths. 
 

Graphite Solutions for the Aluminium Industry

Pyrotek supplies graphite products for molten metal handling, degassing, and casting operations throughout the global aluminium industry. Available products include:
  • Degassing shafts
  • Degassing rotors
  • Graphite rings
  • Flux injection tubes
  • Custom casting components
These products are engineered to deliver reliable performance while helping maintain aluminum cleanliness and casting quality.

Pyrotek graphite degassing rotor

Graphitization and Heat Treatment Services

Pyrotek's proprietary furnace and processing technology delivers exceptionally consistent heat profiles at temperatures from 1000 to 2850 C. We are also capable of scaling from research and development projects (< 1 kg) to continuous production (> 4000 MT / year), while using clean, renewable and low-cost hydropower. Services include: 
 

Industrial Graphitization

Pyrotek provides toll graphitization services for customers requiring consistent, repeatable conversion of carbon-based materials into high-performance graphite. We support:
  • Synthetic graphite materials
  • Natural graphite materials
  • Carbon precursor materials
  • Specialty carbon products

Our in-house lab can also help you achieve targeted material characteristics and performance requirements.

  

Thermal Purification Services

Pyrotek offers high-temperature thermal purification services for applications requiring ultra-high purity powders, including both graphite and non-graphite materials. Our processing technology helps customers achieve:
  • Reduced impurities
  • Improved material consistency
  • Enhanced product performance
  • Repeatable manufacturing results

 

Carburization Services

Pyrotek's proprietary furnace technology supports processing materials in a carbon-rich environment. This enables the processing of various carbides, including tungsten carbide, niobium carbide, titanium carbide, and many others. Please reach out to one of Pyrotek's application engineers to discuss your carburization needs. 

 

Battery Anode Materials

Pyrotek has extensive experience processing graphite anode materials used in lithium-ion battery applications. Our capabilities enable the production of battery-grade graphite anode powder. We are capable of supporting both research scale and moderate commercial scale projects. 
 

Want to learn more? Have a Pyrotek application engineer reach out to discuss your application.
