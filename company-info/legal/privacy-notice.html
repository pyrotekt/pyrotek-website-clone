Privacy Notice

Revision Date: 16 July 2026

I. Introduction

This Privacy Notice describes what personal data we process about our business partners, customers, suppliers and business contacts and informs you about your rights in connection with the personal data processing.

In accordance with applicable data protection law, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR); The United Kingdom Data Use and Access Act and UK-GDPR; California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA + CPRA); Australian Privacy Act; Chinese Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL); India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA); Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK); Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD), and others, depending on your location and/or residency, we would like to inform you that:

  • Pyrotek Incorporated (registered in the United States, 705 West 1st Avenue Spokane, WA 99201-3909), and its subsidiaries ("Pyrotek" or "We") are committed to processing your personal data lawfully and responsibly.
  • The company processing your personal data as the controller is the Pyrotek entity indicated in the underlying agreement with you or with the customer, supplier or business partner who you represent, or the Pyrotek entity with which you intend to establish a business relationship (list of all Pyrotek entities can be found at www.pyrotek.com/assets/Data-Protection/Pyrotek-Entities.pdf), together with Pyrotek Incorporated as the parent company – jointly determining the purposes and means of processing of your personal data as joint controllers.

II. Personal Data We Process

We may collect, store and process some or all of the following personal data:

  1. general identification data (e.g. name, position, employer, address, city, region);
  2. contact data (e.g. your e-mail address, telephone number);
  3. communications with Us (e.g. meeting notes, emails, dates and times);
  4. business cooperation data where applicable (for example when you are a sole trader - natural person) (e.g. qualification, order data, performance data, payment data, data on fulfilment of Pyrotek obligations, details of any third-party beneficiaries, billing data, tax information, product data, creditworthiness and any other business relevant information provided in relation to the business relationship with relevant Pyrotek entity).

These categories reflect personal data we processed in the context of our business relationships within the past 12 months.

III. How We Collect Personal Data

We collect personal data directly from you (for example, when you fill out one of our contact forms on the Pyrotek website), from your organization, or publicly available sources, such as your company website or compliance agencies and other entities within our corporate group.

If required information is not provided, we may be unable to fulfil our contractual or legal obligations related to our business relationship. Certain personal data also may be required by local applicable laws or authorities. Failure to provide or allow us to process this information may prevent or delay our ability to accomplish these obligations.

IV. Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing

Your personal data will be processed for the following purposes:

  1. to carry out our obligations to you under the underlying agreement;
  2. to conduct necessary background checks;
  3. to contact you regarding the services and/or products we are providing to you or may be of interest to you; or the services and/or products you are providing to us;
  4. to ensure accurate invoices for our services and/or products;
  5. to pay invoices;
  6. to comply with legal obligations;
  7. to comply with requests from authorities or legal/administrative proceedings;
  8. to ensure accurate and current records where you, your employees or contractors work on or visit our facilities.
  9. to send marketing communications (subject to the consent of the individual, where required by law), with the option to unsubscribe from these communications at any time.

We process your data based on one or more of the following legal bases, depending on the context in which we collect it:

  1. the fulfilment of Pyrotek’s contractual obligations to you or the entity you represent, for example to ensure that invoices are issued or paid correctly, for ensuring that the goods or product purchased are delivered to the correct location or for ensuring you are able to access our premises when required (e.g. audits).
  2. compliance with a legal obligation to which Pyrotek is subject, for example our obligation to maintain customer/supplier records, statutory returns, crime prevention, ensure our compliance with anti-money laundering and anti-bribery legislations, Know Your clients/suppliers procedures (KYC), health and safety obligations while you are on our premises, or to a third party (e.g. to comply with a court order).
  3. Pyrotek’s legimitate interest including maintaining accurate records, ensuring product and service quality, addressing concerns and managing or defending legal claims.

In the past 12 months, we have not “sold” personal data subject to the local applicable data protection law. For purposes of this Notice, “sold” means the disclosure of personal data to a third-party for monetary or other valuable consideration, including disclosure for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising.

V. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share your personal data with authorities or agencies when legally required and with service providers who support our operations, such as IT systems and services providers, entities providing administrative support, including other companies from within our corporate group (e.g. when another Pyrotek entity manages specific process(es) for the group).

Where we do share your data with third parties or other Pyrotek entities, the shared data is limited to the scope necessary and safeguarded by Data Processing Agreements, establishing specified purposes, instructions and appropriate security measures.

As we operate at a global level, we may transfer personal data to countries other than the one in which it was originally collected. When we export your personal data to a different country, we will take steps to ensure that such data exports comply with applicable laws. For example, all transfers outside of the EEA made to countries which are considered by the European Commission to not provide an adequate level of protection of personal data are safeguarded with agreements including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

More details about sharing data can be obtained from the contact point specified in section VIII. below.

VI. Retention Schedule of Your Personal Data

Our general approach is to retain your personal data only for as long as required to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected. We generally retain your personal data for the duration strictly necessary for the management of our relationship with you or with the customer, supplier or business partner who you represent.

However, we may retain personal data for longer periods of time, for instance where we are required to do so in accordance with legal, tax and accounting requirements, or where such data is necessary to establish the existence of a right or contract and to potentially establish, assert or defend against claims. In that case, your personal data will be archived and retained for the duration imposed by applicable law, or for the duration of the applicable statute of limitations.

VII. Your Data Protection Rights

You have certain rights when we process your personal data: (i) the right to receive information on the processing (right to know); (ii) the right of access to personal data; (iii) the right to rectification/correction; (iv) the right to erasure/deletion; (v) the right to restriction/limitation of processing, and where applicable (vi) the right to data portability and (vii) the right to object to the processing of personal data.

If you wish to exercise any of the rights set out above, use contact details in section VIII. below. Please note that:

  • You do not have to pay a fee to access your personal data (or to exercise any of your other rights) and in accordance with the right to equal treatment, we do not discriminate against individuals for exercising any of the privacy rights;
  • We may request information from you to verify your identity and ensure your right to access your personal data (or other rights). This is a security measure to ensure that your personal data is not disclosed to any person who has no right to receive it. We may also contact you to request additional information related to your request in order to expedite our response;
  • We aim to acknowledge receipt of your request within ten days and respond to all reasonable or routine requests within one month. In occasional cases where the request is particularly complex or several requests have been made, it may take longer than one month. In that case, we will notify you;
  • We may have to limit the scope of the data subject's rights, such as in the case of a request to delete data that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons or where fulfilling the request may expose the personal data of another individual.

You have the right to file a complaint with your local supervisory authority at any time. However, We would appreciate the opportunity to resolve your concerns before you turn to the supervisory authority and We encourage you to contact us first.
