About Pyrotek


We are a diverse, global team of highly trained engineers and technical manufacturing experts serving in the best interests of our many industry partners. We strive to do the right thing for our customers and remain their long-term trusted partner.

Our priority is to have our people working and serving in our customers’ plants. We believe a customer is best served when we know them well through face-to-face meetings and collaboration on the plant floor.

Trusted partners go beyond making the right equipment and parts or engineered solutions. Supporting our customers also includes building long-term relationships, developing a deep understanding of their objectives and sharing insights to help them achieve their goals. 

Pyrotek aims to help its customers and partners around the world improve their competitiveness. We provide access to the latest engineered technology from a company that has consistently been at the forefront of innovation.

Today, we’re more than 3,000 people strong, with over 80 locations in more than 35 countries, including manufacturing plants, research & development centers, and local service and engineering teams. We have grown significantly since our founding in 1956.

Our work is inspired by the customers we know well, and we are committed to being honest and genuine in our relationships, as well as curious, innovative, and sound in our approach.

 

Our Mission 

To provide innovative solutions to customer needs utilizing our global resources.

 

Our Vision

To be a leading international company supplying customers worldwide with performance improving technical products, integrated systems, and value-added services.
